Healthy Together

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026 · Contact: healthy-together-support@googlegroups.com

In short: your data is seen only by your squad (at most 15 friends). Your weight and body stats are private. Health data is never sold, used for ads, or shared for marketing. You can delete your account at any time.

1. Who we are

Healthy Together ("the app") is an iPhone app run by Inderpreet Singh, based in Toronto, Canada. It's currently in a friends-only beta.

Person responsible for your personal information (privacy officer): Inderpreet Singh, owner. Contact: healthy-together-support@googlegroups.com.

3. Apple Health

We read steps, workouts and active energy only with your permission, which you can change at any time in the Health app. We don't write to Apple Health. Health data is never used for advertising, sold, or shared with data brokers or marketers. Steps or workouts you typed in by hand are shown but don't earn points.

4. AI food recognition and food lookups

When you use the AI estimate, the meal photo is sent to the Google Gemini API to identify the foods and portions. During the beta we use Gemini's free tier, under which Google may use submitted photos to improve its products. We'll move to Google's paid tier, which doesn't allow that, and update this policy when we do. If you'd rather not send photos, you can log meals by search, barcode or typing instead.

Calories and macros come from our own nutrition table. When a food isn't in it, only the food's name (never your photo or anything about you) is looked up in the public USDA FoodData Central and Open Food Facts databases. If you scan a barcode, only the barcode number is looked up in Open Food Facts. Indian recipe values come from the Indian Nutrient Databank, stored on our server.

A photo taken in Label or Barcode mode is your meal photo: it's kept and your squad sees it, like any meal photo. If you scan an extra label from the review screen, that photo is sent to Gemini to read the numbers and deleted from our server straight after; it's never shown to your squad.

Most meals are logged with a photo taken in the app. You can log up to 4 meals a week without a photo, and any meal picked from search or your saved meals while you have no squad-mates. If you're in a squad, it sees a "No photo" note on those meals.

If we ever use meal photos to improve our own food recognition, it will be opt-in, and you'll be able to withdraw at any time.

5. Sign-in and notifications

If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive your name and email address from them (Apple may give us a private relay address instead). If you turn on notifications, we store your device's push token (deleted when you sign out) and send notifications through Apple's push service as things happen (race alerts and jabs never between 9 pm and 8 am). Each type can be switched off in the app. Each type can be switched off in the app.

6. Where data is stored

On Supabase (database, file storage and server functions), hosted in Canada (AWS ca-central-1). Photos are kept in private storage and shown through links that expire after an hour.

Some data leaves Canada. Meal and label photos you send for an AI estimate are processed by Google (Gemini API) in the United States. If you sign in with Apple or Google, they handle your sign-in, and notifications go through Apple's push service. Data processed outside Canada is covered by the laws of that country, and its courts and authorities may be able to access it.

7. How long we keep it

While your account is active. If you delete your account, your photos are deleted immediately and everything else 7 days later, in case you change your mind.

8. Your choices and rights

  • See and correct your data in the app (Profile and Settings).
  • Hide calorie numbers from yourself, or hide your progress from your squad.
  • Leave your squad at any time.
  • Delete your account: Profile → Settings → Delete account.
  • Ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or ask any question: healthy-together-support@googlegroups.com. We answer within 30 days.
  • Withdraw your consent at any time: turn off Apple Health access or notifications, log meals without the AI estimate, or delete your account.
  • You can also complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, or, in Quebec, the Commission d'accès à l'information.

9. Security and breaches

Access to your data is limited to your squad by database rules, and photos are only reachable through short-lived links. If a breach of your data creates a real risk of significant harm, we'll tell you and the Privacy Commissioner as soon as we can, and keep a record of every breach.

10. Children

The app is for people 14 and over. We ask for your birth year when you sign up and don't let younger people create an account. If we learn that someone under 14 has an account, we'll delete it.

11. Changes

We'll tell you in the app before any change that affects how your data is used.
